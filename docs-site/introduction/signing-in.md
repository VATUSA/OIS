# Signing in

OIS uses **VATSIM Connect** for sign-in — the same account you use to control or fly on the network. There's no separate OIS password.

## How to sign in

1. Click **Sign in with VATSIM** in the top-right of any page.
2. You're sent to VATSIM's authorization page. Approve the request.
3. You're returned to OIS, now signed in as your CID.

Your session lasts 30 days; after that you'll sign in again. Use **Sign out** in the user menu to end it early.

## What OIS reads from VATSIM

At sign-in OIS receives your **CID, name, rating, and email** from VATSIM Connect. It then looks you up in the [VATUSA](/introduction/profile) roster to fill in your home facility, staff roles, and visiting facilities.

## Do I need to sign in?

- **Pilots** — no. The [advisories board](/advisories/board), [flight lookup](/advisories/pilot), and [facility map](/map/facility-map) are public.
- **Controllers / TMU** — yes. Creating or editing flow initiatives requires a signed-in account with the right [permissions](/reference/permissions).

## Trouble signing in?

- If the button does nothing or you get bounced back, your browser may be blocking third-party cookies for the sign-in redirect — allow cookies for the OIS site and try again.
- If you sign in but don't have the tools you expect, it's a **permissions** issue, not a sign-in one — see [Roles & permissions](/reference/permissions).
