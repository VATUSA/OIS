# Your profile & VATUSA

Once you've [signed in](/introduction/signing-in), open **Profile** from the user menu (top-right). It shows who OIS thinks you are, pulled from VATSIM and VATUSA.

## What's on your profile

- **Header** — your name, CID, ATC rating, and email.
- **VATUSA membership** — your home facility (e.g. `ZDC — Washington ARTCC`), whether you're a home controller, when you joined, and when your details last synced.
- **VATUSA roles** — staff positions grouped by facility (e.g. `ZDC: DATM, INS`).
- **Visiting facilities** — the ARTCCs you visit.
- **OIS access** — your OIS role(s), or *Standard member* if you have none.

## Where the data comes from

OIS syncs your VATUSA details automatically:

- **On sign-in** — OIS fetches your current VATUSA record (rating, facility, roles, visits) in the background.
- **Live updates** — when your VATUSA roster changes (a transfer, a new staff position), OIS is notified and refreshes your record.

You don't need to do anything to keep it current.

## "My VATUSA details haven't synced yet"

If your profile shows this message, OIS hasn't pulled your VATUSA record yet. It fills in after your next sign-in once VATUSA sync is enabled for the deployment. Your CID, name, and rating still show from VATSIM in the meantime.

::: tip Roles vs. access
Your **VATUSA roles** (ATM, INS, …) describe your real-world facility position. Your **OIS access** is what you can *do in OIS*. They're related but not the same — see [Roles & permissions](/reference/permissions).
:::
