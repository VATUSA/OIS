# Live traffic

The map draws every online VATSIM aircraft, refreshed continuously. Each is a small glyph rotated to its heading.

## Icons vs. triangles

Use the **Aircraft icons / Triangles** button in the toolbar to switch between:

- **Aircraft icons** — type-specific silhouettes (a heavy looks different from a light).
- **Triangles** — a plain, uniform marker that reads well when the map is dense.

## Hover & click

- **Hover** an aircraft for its callsign, type, origin → destination, altitude, and groundspeed.
- **Click** it to plot its filed route and open the route panel. Click again (or the ✕) to clear it.

Aircraft matched to a selected [FCA](/tmu/fcas) are drawn tinted and numbered in crossing order instead.
