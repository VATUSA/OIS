# Finding a flight

Use the **find flight** box in the map toolbar to jump to any online aircraft.

## Fuzzy search

Start typing and results rank as you go — you don't need the exact callsign:

- **By callsign** — `AAL12` surfaces `AAL12`, `AAL120`, `AAL1234`…
- **By airport** — `KLAX` finds flights to or from Los Angeles.
- **By type** — `B738` finds 737-800s.

Matched characters are highlighted, and each result shows its route (`DEP → ARR`) and type.

## Selecting a result

Pick a result (click, or ↑/↓ then Enter) and the map flies to that aircraft and plots its route. Press Enter with nothing highlighted to jump straight to an exact callsign you typed.

The same search powers the [flight lookup](/advisories/pilot) on the public pilot page.
