# The flow map

The map is the heart of OIS — a live view of VATSIM traffic across the National Airspace System. It's shared by the controller tools and the public [advisories](/advisories/board) view, so everyone sees the same picture.

## What's on it

- **Live traffic** — every online aircraft, drawn as a triangle or an aircraft-type silhouette. See [Live traffic](/map/traffic).
- **ARTCC boundaries** — the center outlines, labelled by id.
- **Flow Constrained Areas** — any active [FCAs](/tmu/fcas), tinted and numbered.
- **Online ATC** (toggle) — airport position badges, TRACON approach areas, and center shading. See [The ATC layer](/map/atc).

## Getting around

- **Zoom** with the scroll wheel or trackpad — it's a smooth, continuous zoom.
- **Pan** by dragging.
- **Home** re-centers on the continental US.
- **Find a flight** with the search box — see [Finding a flight](/map/flight-search).
- **Aircraft icons / Triangles** toggles between aircraft-type silhouettes and plain triangles.

## Clicking a flight

Click any aircraft to plot its filed route (fixes, airways, and procedures resolved from current FAA nav data) and open an info panel with its callsign, type, origin/destination, altitude, and distance.
