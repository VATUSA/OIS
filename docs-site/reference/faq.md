# FAQ

## Do I need an account to use OIS?

Not for the public views — the [advisories board](/advisories/board) and [flight lookup](/advisories/pilot) are open. Controller tools require [signing in](/introduction/signing-in) with VATSIM.

## My VATUSA details are wrong or missing.

They sync from VATUSA automatically on sign-in and when your roster changes. See [Your profile & VATUSA](/introduction/profile). If something's still off, it's a VATUSA record issue rather than an OIS one.

## Why did a release time change?

An **issued** release doesn't change on its own — see [Release times](/tmu/releases). If you saw a time move, it was likely a **proposed** (advisory) time, or a controller revised/compressed the program.
