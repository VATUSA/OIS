# Restrictions & programs

Beyond FCAs and GDPs, OIS carries the everyday traffic-management restrictions a TMU issues.

## What lives here

- **Miles-in-trail / minutes-in-trail** spacing.
- **Ground stops** and other holds.
- Named **programs** and **restrictions** that appear on the public [advisories board](/advisories/board).

Each restriction has a scope and a lifetime; finished ones expire automatically and drop off the board after a grace period.

> Detailed authoring steps are being documented — this page covers what the restrictions system is for.
