# Facility documents

A short list of reference documents — SOPs, LOAs, and similar — shown to any controller who covers
a facility. Each entry is just a **title** and a **URL** (hosted wherever the facility already keeps
its documents); OIS doesn't store the files themselves, just the pointer to them.

::: info Permissions
Requires `facilities.docs.read` to view; `facilities.docs.update` to add, edit, or remove entries
for your facility.
:::
